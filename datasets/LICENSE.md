## Upstream dataset

HUMAID-NER is an adaptation of the HumAID dataset published by the Qatar Computing Research Institute:

https://huggingface.co/datasets/QCRI/HumAID-all

## Changes made

The HUMAID-NER authors added disaster-specific span-level entity annotations, standardized entity and humanitarian-category labels, validated character offsets, stable record identifiers, reproducible data splits, and research-ready Parquet and CSV distributions.
